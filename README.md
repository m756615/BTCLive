# BTCLive Pro v1.6.0

**Real-time Bitcoin price for Android phones and tablets.**

BTCLive Pro puts the live Bitcoin price on screen: in the status bar when there is room, in a readable chip when there is not, and always in the notification shade, lock screen, and a home screen widget.

## Key features
- **Fits the phone you have**: placement accounts for status-bar height, camera cutouts, and whether the clock sits on the left (Pixel and most stock Android phones) or the right (Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei, and others).
- **Drag to place**: move the price anywhere, including under the status bar, and the spot is remembered.
- **Readable fallback**: a solid chip is used when the status bar is too crowded or the phone will not draw inside it, so the price stays visible on any wallpaper.
- **Home screen widget**: the same price on launchers that do not keep overlays.
- **In-app price**: the tracker runs even before overlay permission is granted.
- **5-second updates while you are looking**: refreshes every 5 seconds with the screen on, and once a minute with the screen off. If one market API fails, the next one is used.
- **Price alerts**: target prices with a high-priority notification and a history you can email.
- **52 colors** and dynamic contrast that follows the phone's light or dark status bar.
- **Backup**: export and import settings, position, and alert history as JSON.
- **Start after reboot and after updates**.

Android 8.0 and newer. Phones and tablets, portrait and landscape, including small screens and large display text.

## Setup
1. Open the app. The price starts updating on the home screen immediately.
2. Allow **Notifications** for the shade, lock screen, and alerts.
3. Allow **Display over other apps** to put the price in the status bar. Find BTCLive in the list and set it to Allowed.
4. Open **On-screen position** if the price covers the clock. Auto is the usual spot for your brand. Drag it if you want it somewhere else.
5. Open **Instructions** and set battery to Unrestricted. Xiaomi, Oppo, vivo, Huawei, and similar phones also need Autostart.
6. Optional: add the **BTCLive** home screen widget.
